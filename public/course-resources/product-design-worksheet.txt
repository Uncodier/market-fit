PRODUCT DESIGN FOUNDATIONS — DEMO PRACTICE WORKSHEET

1. DISCOVER
Who are you designing for?
What are they trying to achieve?
What have you observed, rather than assumed?

2. DEFINE
Write one problem statement:
[Person] needs a way to [goal] because [observed obstacle].
How will you know the experience has improved?

3. EXPLORE
Sketch three different solutions before choosing one.
For each solution, name the assumption you need to validate.

4. TEST
Choose a realistic task for a participant.
Observe without leading them toward the answer.
Record what worked, what confused them, and what to change next.

5. REFLECT
What did you learn?
What will you change?
What is the smallest useful next experiment?
